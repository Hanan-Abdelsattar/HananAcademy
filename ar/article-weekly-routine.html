How to build a weekly study routine

  • Time management
  • October 20, 2026
  • 3 min read

Start by writing down your fixed commitments, then pick your free slots. Do not fill every gap; leave room to rest.

Split the big goal into small tasks and give each one a time. Finishing a small task gives you the motivation to continue.

Review your schedule every week and adjust it. A good schedule is one that changes with you.
