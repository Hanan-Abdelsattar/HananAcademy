Accessibility Statement

Our commitment to access for everyone

Our commitment

We aim for this website to conform to WCAG 2.2 level AA.

What we do

  • A skip link at the start of every page.
  • Tidy headings and clear page landmarks.
  • Full keyboard navigation with a clear focus outline.
  • Sufficient colour contrast, a dark mode, and respect for the reduced-motion preference.
  • A written transcript for each video lesson.
  • A full Arabic version (right to left) and a full English version.

Known limitations

Some videos added later may not have captions the moment they are published; the written transcript is always available.

Your feedback

If you meet any difficulty, tell us and we will work to fix it quickly.
