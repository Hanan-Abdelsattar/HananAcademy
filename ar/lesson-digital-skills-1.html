What is a computer and how do we use it?

Digital Skills for Beginners - Lesson 1

The video for this lesson will be added soon. The full written lesson is below and you can start with it now.

Written lesson (transcript)

A computer is a device that helps us write, learn and communicate. It usually has a processing unit, a screen, a keyboard, and a pointing tool such as a mouse or touchpad.

When you switch the computer on, the start screen appears. From there you open programs such as the browser and the text editor. You can open any program by clicking it or searching for its name.

Before you shut the computer down, save your files and then choose “Shut down” from the menu. Avoid holding the power button unless you really have to.

Key points

  • A computer has a device, a screen and a keyboard
  • We control it with keys, a mouse or touch
  • Always save your work before shutting down
