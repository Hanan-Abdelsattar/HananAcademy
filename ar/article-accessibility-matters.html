Why accessibility matters in online learning

  • Accessibility
  • October 10, 2026
  • 5 min read

When a learning website is designed with care, everyone benefits: people using screen readers, people using only a keyboard, and people on a small phone.

The simplest steps are tidy headings, meaningful link text, alternative text for images, and a written transcript for every video.

These practices cost little, yet they open the door to many more learners.
