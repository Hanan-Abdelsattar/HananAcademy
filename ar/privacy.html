Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

1. Data we collect

When you enrol in a course we collect your name and email address. When you write to us we keep your message. This website does not use tracking cookies.

2. How we use it

We use it only to confirm your enrolment, issue your certificate and reply to your messages.

3. Payments

We never see or store your card details. The payment provider processes them under its own policies.

4. Public certificates

When a certificate is verified by its code, its details (name, course and date) are shown to whoever has the code.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with the payment provider and the hosting service as needed to run the service.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data by contacting us.
