FAQ

Answers to the most common questions.

How do I enrol in a course?

Open the course page, type your name and email, then press the pay button. Free courses start straight from the course page.

Are the lessons available as text?

Yes. Every video lesson has a full written transcript under the video. You can read it, listen to it with a screen reader, or print it.

Which payment methods are available?

Payment takes place on a secure page run by the payment provider; bank cards and e-wallets are usually available. We never see or store your card details.

Will I get a certificate?

Yes. When you finish all the lessons, the academy issues a completion certificate with your name, the course title, the date and a unique verification code.

How can I verify a certificate?

Open the Certificates page and type the verification code printed on the certificate. If it is genuine, its details will be shown.

Is the site suitable for screen readers?

Yes. The site is built for accessibility: tidy headings, clear landmarks, full keyboard navigation and a skip link. If you find a problem, tell us and we will fix it.

Can I get a refund?

Contact us within seven days of purchase and we will review your request. (You can edit this policy in build.py.)

How can I contact you?

By WhatsApp, by email, or with the form on the Contact page.

Did not find your answer? Contact us.
